How to weave

One loom, eighteen chambers

  1. Find the twins. Every colour has two spools. Connect them with a single thread.
  2. Stay orthogonal. A thread moves up, down, left or right — never diagonally.
  3. Never cross. Two threads may not share a cell, and a thread may not pass through another colour's spool.
  4. Cover everything. The chamber is woven only when every cell of the loom carries exactly one thread.

Keyboard: focus the loom, use the arrow keys to move, and Enter or Space to start from a spool. The thread closes when it reaches its twin; Escape cancels the draft.

A clean weave without hints earns three stars. One hint earns two; more than one earns a single star.

The daily weave is a fresh 7×7 chamber on your local date. It has its own save and streak and never changes campaign progress.
